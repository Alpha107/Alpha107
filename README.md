## About Me

I am a **Computer Engineering graduate** (BE, First Division — Tribhuvan University, 2025) from Nepal, with hands-on experience in AI, machine learning, robotics, and embedded systems.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware robotics, machine learning, and applied data intelligence. I have built autonomous robots from scratch using Arduino and ESP32, trained deep learning models on real-world datasets, and deployed full-stack platforms serving geographic and civic data across Nepal.

Alongside my engineering work, I was a **Coding Instructor** at Mero Coding Class — teaching AI, Python, Robotics, and Scratch — because explaining something clearly is the deepest form of understanding.

**What I work on:**
- 🤖 Autonomous robots — from obstacle avoidance to fire suppression systems
- 🧠 ML/DL models — computer vision, CNNs, predictive analytics
- 🌍 Geographic intelligence platforms — real-time data mapped to Nepal's 77 districts
- 📡 IoT systems — GSM, ultrasonic, flame, moisture, and RTC sensor integrations
