About Me

I am an MSc Robotics and Artificial Intelligence student at Cardiff Metropolitan University, UK, and a Computer Engineering graduate (BE, First Division, Tribhuvan University, 2025) from Nepal.

My work sits at the intersection of hardware robotics, machine learning, and simulation. I have built autonomous robots from scratch using Arduino and ESP32, trained deep learning models on real-world datasets, and deployed data platforms covering all 77 districts of Nepal. During my Masters I am focusing on reinforcement learning for robot navigation, SLAM and autonomous exploration, and bio-inspired robot design, with search-and-rescue as a recurring application.

Before moving to the UK, I spent a year as a Coding & Robotics Instructor, teaching AI, Python, robotics, and Scratch, because explaining something clearly is the deepest form of understanding.

What I work on:

🤖 Autonomous & balancing robots — obstacle avoidance, self-balancing, wheel-legged and legged locomotion
🧠 Robot learning — Deep Q-Networks, learned navigation policies, adaptive sensing
🗺️ Mobile robotics — SLAM, mapping, planning and exploration in ROS 2 and Gazebo
🦗 Bio-inspired design — insect-inspired walking and jumping robots
👁️ Computer vision & DL — CNNs, OCR, detection and classification
🌍 Geographic intelligence platforms — real-time data mapped to Nepal's 77 districts
📡 IoT systems — GSM, ultrasonic, flame, moisture, and RTC sensor integrations
